Launching my first Claude Code mod, prompt-lift v0.4.0: one button above the prompt box that rewrites a vague draft from the CLAUDE.md and skills your session already loaded, shows the dollars on the band, and gives you the original back on Undo.

What it does:

-> Install from inside a session: /plugin install prompt-lift --marketplace sairam0424/claude-prompt-lift
-> The committed demo press: draft "fix the login bug", done row "fable · 0 cached · 12.3k in · 144 out · $0.18 · +42 words"
-> Four free pre-guards refuse before any model call: slash or shell lines, reply-shaped drafts, under 3 words, over 4000 characters
-> A paid answer is not auto-accepted: a 14-step pass can refuse it with "rewrite too long", "rewrite dropped too much", "added a command or link you did not write" or "named a file that does not exist, kept your prompt"
-> Spend caps refuse before the call (/prompt-lift cap 2.50); the test is named "a press at the session cap is refused before any call and records nothing"
-> Chain fable, sonnet, haiku; every rung is billed: a press that fell through Fable to Sonnet is priced as both. Unpriced models show $?, never $0
-> Undo restores your draft; on a box you edited since Enhance it refuses once and asks you to press again within 10 s
-> /prompt-lift preview <draft> shows what a press would send, no model call; /prompt-lift probe says whether your account can reach each rung
-> One outbound call per press on your own model access; no http.fetch, process.run or timers in the hooks. Instruction files do travel with their absolute paths
-> Honest gap: v0.4.0 shipped two bugs, both found by the catalogue maintainer within a day: a sub-project CLAUDE.md ignored instructionExclude, and two presses in the same instant could drop one from the day ledger. 0.4.1 fixes the first and half of the second; the cross-session race is open and the catalogue PR is blocked.

Four days, 130 commits, 329 test cases at tag v0.4.0 (commit 1aa0b4f); the one demo press on record cost $0.18.
