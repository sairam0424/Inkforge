# GIF Plan — tracehub-mcp v0.12.3 + cost-guard-mcp v0.3.3 Combined Release Article (Merged Fit-Review)

Merged from the two existing per-project plans (`tracehub-mcp-v0-12-3-release/gifs-plan.md`, four picks + eight alternates; `cost-guard-mcp-v0-3-3-release/gifs-plan.md`, four picks + ten alternates). No new GIF search was run. The combined article has twenty `##` sections and one GIF budget of five, so five of the eight previously selected finalists were kept and three (the doctor X-ray, the plug-and-socket, the cockpit pre-flight panel) were demoted to alternates. Every pick below is mapped to an exact `##` heading of the combined article, and every direct media URL in this document — the 5 picks and all 21 carried alternates — was re-checked live by this consolidator on 2026-10-10 rather than carried on trust, because Giphy's oEmbed titles are frequently generic (three of the five picks are credited to the bare `GIPHY` account or carry a title that names the uploader, not the content).

## Verification method (independently re-run, not taken on trust)

Source plans: candidates were discovered from live `giphy.com` search/explore HTML → `https://giphy.com/services/oembed?url=<page_url>` returned the canonical direct media URL + `author_name` → `curl -sI` on the direct URL returned `HTTP/2 200` + `content-type: image/gif` + `content-length` → the `.gif` was downloaded → `ffprobe`'d for resolution and frame count → `ffmpeg`/Pillow decoded the mid-sequence frame plus a contact strip → inspected visually for burned-in text, logos and watermarks.

Merge pass (this document): on 2026-10-10 the consolidator re-ran `curl -sI` against all 26 direct URLs that appear in this document (5 picks, 21 alternates) in one batch. Every one returned `HTTP/2 200`, `content-type: image/gif`, and a `content-length` byte-identical to the figure recorded in the source plan it came from (501,579 / 88,128 / 412,068 / 2,770,645 / 24,927 for the five picks), so the bytes on the CDN today are the bytes that were frame-decoded and inspected in the source plans. The frame-level descriptions below are carried from those plans' decoded mid-frames; the alt text in each embed line is written from those pixels, not from the Giphy title. The oEmbed `author_name` values are carried from the source plans' lookups. Nothing flagged in either source plan as unverified, over the ~4 MB size cap, or carrying burned-in third-party text is carried forward as a usable option.

Section mapping rationale: the combined article's six candidate beats were the shared discoverability-drift frame, tracehub's backend expansion, tracehub's triage/hardening, cost-guard's cost cap/refusal, cost-guard's secret redaction, and the stale-registry surfaces. With five slots, the shared opening frame was folded into the stale-registry slot (the "Where to get them" tables are where the article itself says the drift is visible), the triage beat was dropped in favour of hardening (the container-image/Trivy/SHA-pin section is the article's clearest "guard behind every defect" moment), and the remaining four beats each got one GIF. Placement order follows article order: section 3, 7, 10, 11, 18 of twenty.
